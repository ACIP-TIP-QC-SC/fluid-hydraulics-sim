About Us

The American Concrete Institute Philippines Technological Institute of the Philippines Quezon City Student Chapter (ACIP T.I.P. QC SC) is the Premier Civil Engineering Student Organization at T.I.P. QC.

Mission

To help our members grow into competent, ethical, and future-ready civil engineers by providing academic and professional services, technical seminars, and hands-on workshops guided by ACI standards and best practices in concrete design and construction.

Vision

To be the leading student chapter of ACI Philippines, known for producing civil engineers who build safe, durable, and sustainable concrete structures for the nation.
